# Code of Conduct

Open Flow welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Discuss code and ideas without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, sexualized conduct, threats, doxxing and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable. Respect boundaries and stop unwanted contact.

This policy applies in repository issues, reviews, discussions and project-related community spaces. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact methods on https://github.com/Lord-shaban; do not publish private details. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality where possible, and may warn, remove content, restrict participation or ban participants based on severity. Maintainers follow the same standards. Appeals may be sent privately to the owner.

Our goal is a safe, productive community with clear, proportionate enforcement.
